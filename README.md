## Why They Need a Better Website / AI Automation

### 1. 24/7 Online Booking is Their Whole Model — But It Can Leak
Sim Golf Now advertises online booking around the clock. If their booking flow is clunky, unconfirmed, or not followed up with automated reminders, they're losing walk-in and late-night impulse bookings.
**Pitch:** AI-powered booking confirmation + automated reminder sequences (24hr / 2hr before tee time) that reduce no-shows and fill cancellations automatically.

### 2. Corporate & Event Revenue Is Untapped on Their Site
Private simulator bays are perfect for bachelor parties, corporate outings, and team-building — high-ticket group bookings that are likely under-marketed.
**Pitch:** A dedicated events/group booking landing page + automated follow-up funnel for corporate inquiry forms.

### 3. Membership Retention
Simulator venues live and die by recurring memberships. Most don't have automated win-back campaigns for lapsed members.
**Pitch:** Automated SMS/email win-back sequences triggered when a member hasn't booked in 30+ days.

### 4. Trackman Is a Premium Differentiator — Are They Using It in Marketing?
Trackman is the gold standard in golf tech. Prospects searching "golf lessons Fort Mill" or "golf simulator Charlotte" may not know they offer it.
**Pitch:** SEO-optimized landing pages targeting Trackman + location keywords, built and managed by PalmettoAI.
